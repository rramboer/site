## Audit findings — what the main plan gets wrong or misses

1. **Day 3 (North Cascades) doesn't work as written.** The timeline has you at the Hertz counter (6:00 AM) before you arrive at the airport Link station (6:10 AM) — and Hertz is actually at the off-airport Rental Car Facility, which adds a shuttle ride. Bigger problem: Maple Pass + Blue Lake + Rainy Lake is 7.5–8.5 hrs of hiking that can't start before ~11:30 AM, putting you back in Seattle near midnight. Also, "all 3 hikes from Rainy Pass parking lot" is wrong — **Blue Lake has its own trailhead** ~1.5 mi west of Washington Pass; only Maple Pass and Rainy Lake share the Rainy Pass lot. Fix below: Uber direct to the rental facility, Maple Pass + Rainy Lake as the core, Blue Lake optional.
2. **Day 1 dinner/sunset conflict.** A 7:00 PM dinner can't precede the 8:09 PM Kerry Park sunset. Fix: dinner at 5:30, Kerry Park by 7:30.
3. **Tilth closed permanently in 2020** — remove from the Day 1 dinner list.
4. ~~Pink Door~~ — **resolved: booked Il Bistro (93 Pike St) Sat Aug 22, 5:30 PM.** Pink Door had no Saturday availability, and it's closed Sun/Mon, so no other trip day worked. Note for future reference: Michelin does not cover Seattle, so no restaurant in the city has a star.
5. **Day 7 (La Push) is timed to the wrong tide.** Second Beach at 1 PM = almost exactly high tide (+7.7 ft at 1:45 PM) — minimal beach, no pools. The tide falls all afternoon to +1.3 ft by 7:30 PM. Fix: beaches 2:30–5:30, dinner at River's Edge, First Beach (drive-up, no dark trail) for the 8:07 sunset.
6. **Day 11/12 tidepooling is on the wrong day.** NOAA: the only decent daytime low of the coast stay is **Tue Sep 1 ~9:20 AM (+1.4 ft)**. Wed is +2.2 ft (pools mostly drowned), Thu worse; the truly low tides are near midnight all week. Fix: leave Portland at 7 AM Tuesday, tidepool first, Tillamook loop after.
7. **Sep 5 is the Saturday of Labor Day weekend** — SEA security will be busy at dawn; the departure morning needs ~30 more minutes. Rainier on Sep 3–4 will also be busier than normal.
8. **Day 9 arrival math**: Aberdeen → Portland is ~2 hr 40 min, so Portland arrival is realistically ~12:45–1:00 PM, not 12:30.
9. **Piedmont Café (Day 2)** is a coffee-and-pastry café on First Hill (1215 Seneca St), not a full sit-down breakfast.

**Decision: Maple Pass Loop + Blue Lake (Rainy Lake dropped).** ~7 hrs of hiking — the roadside stops get trimmed to pay for it.

- 9:30 AM — Gorge Overlook + Diablo Lake Vista, quick pullouts only
- 10:15 AM — Arrive Rainy Pass
- 10:30 AM — **Maple Pass Loop** (7.5 mi, ~2,000 ft, brisk pace 4 hr)
- 2:30 PM — Finish; drive ~4 mi east to the **separate** Blue Lake trailhead
- 2:45 PM — **Blue Lake** (4.4 mi RT, ~1,050 ft, brisk pace 2 hr)
- 4:45 PM — Finish
- 5:00 PM — Washington Pass Observation Site (~1 mi east, 15 min)
- 5:15 PM — Drive west (~1h40m to Burlington)
- 7:00 PM — **Dinner stop on I-5** (Burlington/Mount Vernon), ~35 min. SR-20 between Washington Pass and Burlington is empty — Marblemount closes early, then nothing until I-5
- 8:45 PM — Seattle hotel; car in garage

**Pace assumption:** the above runs Maple Pass at 4 hr and Blue Lake at 2 hr — brisk but reasonable for fit hikers. At a moderate pace (4.5 / 2.5 hr) plus a longer dinner, everything shifts ~90 min later and you're back at 10:15 PM.

**Trailhead pass:** Rainy Pass and Blue Lake are USFS (Okanogan-Wenatchee NF), not park land, so they need a Northwest Forest Pass — **the America the Beautiful pass satisfies this**. Display it signed on the dashboard. North Cascades NP itself is fee-free.

**Cut from the original:** Rainy Lake, Trail of the Cedars, Ladder Creek Falls, Ross Lake Overlook. The original called for all three hikes (7.5–8.5 hr) plus seven roadside stops, which put the finish at 7:30–8:15 PM and a midnight Seattle arrival — three hours of dark mountain driving after a 4:45 AM wake.

**Fallback if you're behind:** Blue Lake is the one to drop. Missing it costs 2.5 hr; missing the drive home costs the night.

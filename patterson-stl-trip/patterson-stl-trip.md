## Good to know

- **Zoo admission is free for everyone.** Parking is $20 and cashless — pay on the way out by QR code.
- **Sea lion show seats are first-come at the booth, released 45 minutes before the show.** Someone needs to grab seven around 10:15.
- **Magic House Summerween** is that weekend, included with admission — costumes or swimsuits both count.
- The **butterfly conservatory is warm and humid**, so it's not a place to cool off. Bright clothes make butterflies land on you.

## If it rains

Friday and Saturday are already indoors. If Sunday turns, the **Science Center** is free and five minutes across Forest Park. Any morning that falls apart: come home, eat, get in the pool.
